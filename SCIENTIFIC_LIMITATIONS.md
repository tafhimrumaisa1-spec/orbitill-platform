# Scientific limitations

Updated 2026-11-15. These limitations are part of the product, not fine print.

## What the Barind demonstration establishes

- A verified offline NASA POWER daily bundle supplies precipitation, temperature, humidity, wind and solar-radiation context for the reference grid cell.
- EarthSync preserves dates, units, source identity, spatial support and retrieval metadata.
- AquaSense uses that weather in a deterministic crop-water calculation.
- The same rainfall multiplier and seasonal irrigation allowance are applied to three declared pathways.
- A changed assumption produces a new model-run identity and recalculated water outputs.

## What it does not establish

- It does not recommend Boro rice, wheat or mustard for a real farm.
- It does not predict yield, profit, groundwater response or household outcome.
- It does not demonstrate field validation, adoption, savings, accuracy or impact.
- NASA POWER is gridded environmental context, not field rainfall, soil moisture or groundwater measurement.
- The demonstration soil-water values and irrigation efficiency are supplied hypothetical inputs.
- Wheat stages use a published South Asian analogue. The Boro and mustard calendars remain visibly unverified planning placeholders.
- SoilBrain currently lacks a complete evidence-supported Barind calendar and soil-threshold pack. Missing evidence remains conditional.
- The current comparison isolates water implications. Economics and multi-objective ranking are intentionally unavailable until source-compatible local inputs exist.

## Regional boundaries

- **Barind/Rajshahi:** conditional research demonstration.
- **Coastal Bangladesh:** environmental priority area; crop recommendations are unavailable without dated soil or irrigation-water salinity, local crop calendars and economics.
- **Iowa:** separate research story with verified NASA POWER context; not connected to Bangladesh assumptions.
- **Global:** responsible exploration and capability routing only; no universal agronomic recommendation.

## Appropriate interpretation

Use the output to ask which pathway deserves local investigation and what evidence must be collected next. Do not use it as planting, irrigation, financial or groundwater-management advice.
