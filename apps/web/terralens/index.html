                    About

                    What this instrument does — and refuses to do

                    TerraLens resolves a coordinate or place to a country, an administrative chain and a regional readiness state. It selects which NASA products may inform an analysis and states their native footprint. It does not fetch environmental values, store crop rules or validate farms.

                    • Boundaries are cartographic: geoBoundaries (CC0 / CC BY 3.0 IGO) and Natural Earth (public domain). They are not cadastral.
                    • NASA layers are gridded or image products. An SMAP 9 km cell, an IMERG 11 km cell and a 30 m HLS pixel are not measurements of a field.
                    • Readiness stops at research_pilot. validated_with_evidence exists in the shared contract but is never emitted here.
                    • Ownership: EarthSync fetches environmental series, SoilBrain owns crop and soil rules, AquaSense owns water state, RootShift compares rotations, FutureFields computes economics.

                    No API keys. No analytics. Optional raster tiles come directly from NASA GIBS. MapLibre GL JS 6.18.0 (BSD-3-Clause) is vendored locally.
